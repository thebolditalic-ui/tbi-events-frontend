Facebook Invites

Invite people who reacted to a post to follow The Bold Italic. Drag the button to your bookmarks bar, then on a Facebook post's reactions list, click it. It invites up to 100 at a time (with a pause partway through), only clicks exact "Invite" buttons, skips Invited and Following, and logs each run below. Facebook caps invites near 500/day, so keep it to a few runs a day.

Bookmarklet · install the invite button
Install once: drag this to your bookmarks bar → 🔁 TBI Invite (100)
Then open a Facebook post, click its reaction count to open the "People who reacted" list (pick a reaction tab if you like), and click TBI Invite. Keep that tab open and in front while it runs.
